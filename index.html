I work with founders, engineers, product and sales to find what is truly unique about a company, read the market, and position the company where it can win. That becomes the marketing foundation: brand, positioning, messaging and narrative strategy.

From there I build what it takes to test the story and land the first customers — sales enablement and sales process, the website and SEO, events and social — and help the sales team qualify and convert prospects. I also set up internal and external comms, so the team and the market hear the same story.

I’ve done this for seven AI B2B products, from concept to first customer.
