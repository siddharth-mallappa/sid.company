overview

EasyDocument AI turns scattered paperwork into structured, usable data. The technology was strong; the offering was complicated. I joined as founding marketeer to make it simple to understand and simple to buy.

One brand for every product: a flow mark of three lines, the moment scattered paperwork becomes structured data.

One brand, three clear products, and a wedge that opens the account.

what i did

I restructured the offering into three products: AI Contracts, AI FinOps and AI EXIM.

EasyContracts became the wedge: the simplest product for a buyer to say yes to, and the one that opens the account for the rest.
