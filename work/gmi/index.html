overview

Great Manager Institute measures how effective managers are. Its analytics platform turns survey data into a single Managerial Effectiveness Score, benchmarked against the industry, and connects it to culture, retention, engagement, mentorship and leadership.

The challenge was to make a dense dataset readable for the people who act on it: a leadership team that needs the headline in one screen, and HR teams that need to dig into why.

my role

  • Positioning: segmentation, pricing and go-to-market strategy
  • Storytelling: the walkthrough that takes a buyer from the headline score to the leadership pipeline
  • Product: shaping the platform and its screens, from the cockpit to the explorers

how it works

  1. The cockpitOne screen with the scores that matter: the Managerial Effectiveness Score against the industry benchmark, culture, manager retention, engagement, mentorship and leadership. AI-written insights sit on top, like retention boosts and recurring sentiments.
  2. The explorersClick any score to go deeper. The MES, culture and manager-retention explorers break each number down and compare it with the last survey.
  3. Focus viewsSlice by group, such as Star Performers, and see which drivers set them apart: balanced work, respect, care for the individual.
  4. Leadership pipelineFind high-potential and ready talent for open positions, the training needs they share, and the skills each candidate still needs to build.
