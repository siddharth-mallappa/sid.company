Townsquare

Co-founder · 2022 – 2023

Pitch deck. Use the arrow keys or swipe. Open full screen

overview

Local communities are scattered across WhatsApp groups, Facebook groups, Discord servers and Instagram pages. People can’t find them, and they can’t reach the people nearby.

Townsquare put communities at the centre: one place for the people, communities and events in your city, sorted by distance and introduced through friends.

my role

Co-founder and COO. I designed the product and led customer development:

  • Talking to users
  • Cold emailing to win the first ones
  • Refining the offer and the pitch until they got replies

takeaways

It taught me to spot a tarpit idea early, which I have used on every product since.
